## Brand & Style

The design system projects precision, engineering rigor, and high-craft digital artistry. Built for elite creative technologists, systems architects, and product designers, it pairs a dark obsidian foundation with luminous electric blue and cyan focal points. The aesthetic synthesizes high-end technical minimalism with subtle glassmorphic depth, creating a tactile canvas that feels computational, calm, and deliberate.

### Design Principles
- **Monochrome Mastery:** Neutral surfaces rely on deep carbon and obsidian values rather than muddy grays, allowing crisp light to emerge naturally.
- **Architectural Radiance:** Electric blue and cyan accents are used sparingly as laser-focused cues—signaling interactivity, status, and narrative climax without visual fatigue.
- **Micro-Precision:** Fine 1px borders, subtle inner highlights, and monospaced meta-labels convey intentionality and technical exactitude.

The palette establishes an ultra-deep, cold-cast dark theme by default, complemented by a pristine, gallery-grade light mode.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy communicates crisp engineering standards. `Geist` delivers neutral, hyper-legible geometric clarity across body and display contexts, while `JetBrains Mono` handles metadata, tags, time intervals, code snippets, and structural subheads.

- **Tracking Rules:** Large display headings use tightened letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) to maintain optical density. Monospaced uppercase labels apply wide tracking (`0.08em`) to guarantee legibility at small scale.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Emphasize project titles and metrics by pairing `display-hero` with adjacent `label-caps` in secondary cyan tones.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded on a 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container constraints to maintain optimal scanning paths for portfolios and case studies.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Max container width `1200px`, 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, outer `margin: 3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, outer `margin: 2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, outer `margin: 1.25rem`.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical cadence follows dynamic multiples of `space-md` (1rem). Component interiors use `space-sm` to `space-md` for high information density, while editorial section transitions leverage `space-xl` and extended margins for breathability.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is articulated through tonal layering and luminescence rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **Surface Layering:** Elements step forward through subtle increments in surface luminance (`#050608` → `#0C0E14` → `#141721`), reinforced by a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Subtle Glassmorphism:** Floating navigation bars and modal sheets use a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with a 75% alpha fill of the underlying surface tone.
- **Luminescent Accent Glows:** Active cards and focused interactive controls cast a multi-stop ambient radiance: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.35), 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Inner Light Catchers:** Top borders of elevated cards feature a pseudo-element 1px gradient line fading from transparent to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` to transparent, simulating top-down light reflection.

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on soft, calculated corners that balance technological discipline with modern software ergonomics.

- **Standard Base:** `0.25rem` (4px) for controls, inputs, chips, and code tags.
- **Containers & Cards:** `0.5rem` (8px) for cards, preview frames, and modal panels.
- **Inner Nested Elements:** When placing items within cards, follow the concentric radius formula: nested radius equals outer container radius minus container padding.
- **Pills:** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, live badge indicators, and segmented filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Linear gradient fill from `#2979FF` to `#00E5FF`, pure black text (`#050608`) in `Geist` Semi-Bold, 0.25rem radius. Hover triggers an expanded soft cyan glow and a subtle 1% scale-up with `transition: all 200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border and white text. On hover: border tints to `#00E5FF`, text gains accent color, background shifts to `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.04)`.

### Cards & Project Showcase
- Built with `Surface Level 1` background, 1px subtle border, and 0.5rem radius.
- **Interactive State:** Hover activates an ultra-fine border transition to `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.5)` alongside the signature ambient glow. Nested preview images or mockups scale slightly (`scale(1.02)`) under smooth GPU transitions.

### Chips & Badges
- Rendered using `label-code` typography.
- Neutral tech stacks use a transparent black fill with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` border and muted text.
- Active/Featured tags incorporate a pulsating 6px circular dot in `#00E5FF` accompanied by `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.1)` background fill.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls use `Surface Level 2` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Placeholder text in tertiary gray.
- **Focus:** Outline zeroed; bordered by `#00E5FF` with a continuous `0 0 0 2px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.2)` ring.
- Checkboxes and radios display custom micro-borders and light up with solid cyan fills upon selection.

### Terminal & Metric Blocks
- Special portfolio component styled as an interactive code snippet or live metric block: dark monospace terminal window, faint dot grid background, syntax-highlighted labels, and a live ping status beacon.
